# Broccoli Soup

## Ingredients

- Onion
- Potatos
- Broccoli
- Vegetable broth
- Cooking cream
- Salt
- Pepper


## Instructions

- Peel and slice the onion and potatoes. Cut the whole broccoli into pieces, including the stem.
- Bring the broth to a boil and add the onion, potatoes and broccoli. Bring to a boil and let it cook for about 10 minutes or until the vegetables are soft. Blend the soup with a hand blender or in a food processor.
- Pour the soup back into the saucepan, add the cooking cream, bring to a boil and season with salt and pepper.
- To serve: Serve the soup with bread and crayfish tails.
